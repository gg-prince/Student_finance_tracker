About Traksta

Why it exists

Traksta helps students see where their money goes. Write down each purchase (lunch, a textbook, a bus pass), and Traksta adds it all up, shows your biggest spending category, charts the last seven days, and tells you how much of your budget is left.

How to use it

  1. Open Settings and set a spending cap, and your currencies if you need something other than the defaults.
  2. Add each purchase on the Add page. The form tells you right away if something is wrong, like an impossible date or an amount with three decimals.
  3. Check the Home dashboard to see your total, your top category, the last seven days, and what is left under your cap.
  4. Use Records to search, sort, edit or delete anything you entered.
